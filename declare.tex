{\bf Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools}} \\
\vspace*{0.3 cm}

\noindent
I hereby declare that generative artificial intelligence tools were used in a supportive capacity during the preparation of this thesis, namely:
\begin{itemize}
    \item[-] \textit{ChatGPT} and \textit{Google Gemini} were used as auxiliary tools during parts of the implementation, analysis, and report preparation process,
    \item[-] their use was limited to tasks such as conceptual support, code-related assistance, formulation improvement, structural refinement, and language editing,
    \item[-] all generated suggestions and outputs were critically reviewed, validated, and revised by me before inclusion in this work,
    \item[-] all implementation decisions, methodological choices, experimental results, interpretations, and the final written presentation were independently examined and approved by me, and
    \item[-] full responsibility for the accuracy, originality, academic integrity, and final content of this thesis remains solely with me.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The use of artificial intelligence tools served exclusively as supportive assistance and did not replace independent scientific reasoning or authorial responsibility.\\\\\\\\
